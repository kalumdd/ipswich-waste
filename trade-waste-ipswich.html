Trade waste collection: what to expect

If you produce waste in the course of your work, it is trade waste, even if you are working in someone's home. You cannot take it to a household recycling centre for free, and both you and your customer have a duty of care to make sure it goes to a licensed site.

We collect trade waste across Ipswich in whatever way suits the job: a skip on site, a grab lorry for heavy spoil, a caged lorry for a quick wait-and-load, or regular collections from your yard or van. Trade account customers get priority booking and one monthly invoice.

Every collection comes with a waste transfer note, so you can show customers and inspectors where your waste went. That paperwork matters: fly-tipping fines and prosecutions often trace back to traders who handed waste to unlicensed carriers.

Trade waste and the law

Your duty of care

Anyone who produces business waste must take reasonable steps to make sure it is handled by a registered carrier and taken to an authorised site. Keep your transfer notes for two years.

Carrying your own waste

If you carry your own construction waste in your van, you need to register as a waste carrier with the Environment Agency. Taking it to a licensed site will usually involve a gate fee.

Household recycling centres

Suffolk's recycling centres are for household waste. Traders bringing work waste can be turned away or charged, and vans may need a permit.

Hazardous waste

Paint, oils, solvents, asbestos and fluorescent tubes are hazardous and need consignment notes. Keep them out of general waste and ask us for a separate collection.

Frequently asked questions

Can tradespeople use the tip for free?

No. Household recycling centres are for household waste. Trade waste needs a commercial service.

Do I need a waste carrier licence?

If you carry your own construction or demolition waste, you must register with the Environment Agency as a waste carrier.

Can I open a trade account?

Yes. Trade accounts include priority booking and monthly invoicing.

Will I get transfer notes for my customers?

Yes. Every collection comes with a waste transfer note you can share with customers.

Do you collect from my yard?

Yes, on a regular schedule or on request.

Can you take old boilers and radiators?

Yes. Metal items can go for scrap recycling. See scrap metal collection.
