Scrap metal collection: what to expect

Metal is one of the most valuable materials in the waste stream, and almost all of it can be recycled again and again. Steel, iron, copper, brass, aluminium and lead are all collected, sorted and sent to be melted down and made into new products.

We collect scrap metal across Ipswich from households clearing out garages and sheds, plumbers and heating engineers with old boilers and radiators, builders with steel and cable, and businesses with old racking, machinery and equipment.

Under the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013, scrap metal must be collected by a licensed dealer or collector, and cash payments for scrap are illegal. Using a licensed collector means you know your metal is recycled properly and not fly-tipped.

Scrap metal tips

Separate valuable metals

Copper, brass and aluminium are worth more than steel. Keeping them separate can reduce or offset collection costs on larger loads.

Licensing matters

Only use a licensed scrap metal collector. Unlicensed collectors operating door to door may not dispose of metal legally.

Hazardous parts

Fridges, batteries and items containing oil need special treatment. Tell us about them so they go to the right facility.

Trade and site collections

Plumbers, electricians and builders can book regular collections or keep a dedicated metal skip on site.

Frequently asked questions

Do you pay cash for scrap?

No. Cash payments for scrap metal are illegal under the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013.

Can you take old radiators and boilers?

Yes, from homes and from heating engineers' jobs.

Do you collect cable?

Yes, including cable from rewires and strip-outs.

Can you take a whole car?

Not at this time. End-of-life vehicles go to an authorised treatment facility.

Do you collect from building sites?

Yes, including metal skips on site.

Is all the metal recycled?

Yes. Metal is sorted by type and sent for recycling.
