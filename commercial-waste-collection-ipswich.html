Commercial waste collection: what to expect

Every business in England has a legal duty to deal with its waste properly. You cannot put business waste in a household bin or take it to a household recycling centre, and you need a waste transfer note for every collection. A commercial waste contract takes care of all of this.

We supply bins sized for your business, collect them on a schedule that suits your opening hours, and send the waste for recycling and recovery. Pubs and restaurants in the town centre, offices on the Waterfront, shops along Westgate Street and Carr Street, and workshops on the industrial estates all have different needs, so we build each contract around the business.

From 31 March 2025, most businesses in England with 10 or more employees have had to separate dry recycling and food waste from general waste under the Simpler Recycling rules. Micro-firms with fewer than 10 employees must follow the same rules from 31 March 2027. We can set you up with the right bins now so you are compliant.

Your duty of care for business waste

Use a registered carrier

Business waste must only be handed to a registered waste carrier. You can check any carrier on the Environment Agency's public register.

Keep your paperwork

You need a waste transfer note describing your waste, which must be kept for at least two years. With a contract, an annual transfer note usually covers regular collections.

Separate your recycling

Under Simpler Recycling, businesses with 10 or more employees must keep dry recycling and food waste apart from general waste. Smaller businesses must comply from 31 March 2027.

Store waste securely

Bins should be lidded and stored where waste cannot blow away or be scattered. Lockable bins help prevent fly-tipping into your bins by others.

Reduce what you throw away

Recycling streams are usually cheaper than general waste, so moving waste from general to recycling bins can cut your bill. A waste audit shows where the savings are.

Frequently asked questions

Can I put business waste in my household bin?

No. Business waste must go through a commercial collection. Using a household bin or the tip for business waste can lead to a fine.

Do I have to separate recycling?

If you have 10 or more employees, yes, under Simpler Recycling rules in force since 31 March 2025. Businesses with fewer than 10 employees must comply from 31 March 2027.

How often can bins be collected?

From daily to fortnightly. Food waste is usually collected at least weekly.

Can I change my bin size later?

Yes. If your business grows or your waste changes, we can swap bins or adjust collection days.

Do you collect from pubs and restaurants early in the morning?

Yes. We can schedule collections to avoid busy service times and noise restrictions.

Will I get a waste transfer note?

Yes. You receive one at the start of your contract, covering your regular collections.
