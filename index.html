One local company for every kind of waste

Most people need a waste company two or three times a year: a skip for a kitchen refit, a collection for an old sofa, a clearance when a relative moves into care. Businesses need it every week. Ipswich Waste covers all of it, from a single mattress to a full roll-on roll-off programme on a building site.

We are based in Ipswich and work across the town and the surrounding area, from Felixstowe and Woodbridge to Hadleigh, Needham Market and Stowmarket. Our vehicles and team are local, so we can usually deliver skips the next working day and collect rubbish the same or next day.

Everything we collect goes to licensed facilities. Rubble is crushed into recycled aggregate, metal and cardboard are recycled, green waste is composted and good furniture is passed on for reuse. Items that need special handling, such as fridges, sofas, tyres and asbestos, are kept separate and sent to the right places.

Not sure which service you need? Tell us what the waste is and roughly how much there is, and we will recommend the cheapest way to deal with it. Sometimes that is a skip, sometimes a skip bag, and sometimes a quick collection where we do all the lifting.

Choosing a waste removal company

Check they are a registered waste carrier

Anyone who collects waste for money must be registered with the Environment Agency. You can check a carrier's registration on the public register. If waste you handed over is fly-tipped, you can be fined too.

Ask where the waste goes

A legitimate company will tell you which licensed facility your waste is taken to, and give you a waste transfer note or receipt.

Be wary of very cheap quotes

Prices far below the market often mean waste is being dumped. Proper disposal has real costs, especially for items like sofas, fridges and tyres.

Pick the right service

Skips suit projects over several days. Grab hire suits heavy soil and rubble. Man and van suits bulky items you do not want to lift. Choosing well can halve the cost.

Frequently asked questions

Which waste service do I need?

For ongoing projects, a skip. For heavy soil and rubble, grab hire. For furniture and bulky items, a man and van collection. For small amounts, a skip bag. Tell us about the job and we will recommend the cheapest option.

How quickly can you collect in Ipswich?

Rubbish collections are often same or next day. Skips are usually delivered the next working day, or after a few days if a road permit is needed.

Are you a licensed waste carrier?

Yes. We are registered with the Environment Agency, and every collection comes with a waste transfer note.

Do you recycle?

Yes. Waste is sorted at licensed facilities and as much as possible is recycled or reused.

Do you take sofas, fridges and mattresses?

Yes, but not in skips. They are collected separately and sent to specialist facilities.

Do you work with businesses?

Yes. We provide commercial waste contracts, trade accounts, clearances and specialist collections for businesses of all sizes.

What areas do you cover?

Ipswich and the surrounding area, including Felixstowe, Woodbridge, Kesgrave, Hadleigh, Needham Market and Stowmarket.

Do I need a permit for a skip?

Only if it goes on the road or pavement. We apply to Suffolk County Council for you.
