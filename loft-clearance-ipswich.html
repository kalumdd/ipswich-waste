Loft clearance: what to expect

Lofts are hard to clear yourself. The hatch is small, the boards are uneven, the light is poor and everything has to come down a ladder. It is one of the jobs people put off longest, until a house move, a loft conversion or new insulation means it has to be done.

We clear lofts across Ipswich, from bungalows with shallow roof spaces to Edwardian houses with full attics. Our team works in pairs, one in the loft and one on the landing, passing items down safely and protecting your stairs and carpets on the way out.

If you are having a loft conversion or insulation fitted, we can clear the space a few days before the builders arrive, including old insulation and boards if the contractor needs bare joists.

Clearing a loft safely

Check for asbestos

Some homes built before 2000 have asbestos in loft insulation, water tanks or pipe lagging, particularly loose vermiculite-type insulation. If you are unsure, do not disturb it. We can arrange an asbestos survey first.

Do not stand between joists

Ceilings below lofts are not designed to take weight. Our team works only on boarded areas or uses crawl boards.

Sort before you go up

If you want to keep certain items, describe them or label boxes. We bring everything down and you choose what stays.

Before a conversion

Builders usually need the loft completely empty. Book the clearance a week or so before work starts to avoid delays.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a loft clearance take?

Most lofts take two to four hours, depending on how full they are and how easy the access is.

Can you remove old insulation?

Yes, mineral wool insulation can be bagged and removed. If it might contain asbestos, it must be tested first.

Will you protect my stairs and carpets?

Yes. We lay protective sheets along the route from the hatch to the door.

Can you clear a loft before a conversion?

Yes. We regularly clear lofts for conversion builders across Ipswich.

Do you take old water tanks?

Yes, where it is safe. Asbestos cement tanks need specialist removal.

What happens to the items?

Reusable items are donated, the rest is sorted for recycling.
